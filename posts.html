The 2026 Total Solar Eclipse in Spain


Published: August 2026



On August 12, 2026, Spain will experience one of the most exciting astronomical events in many years.


A total solar eclipse happens when the Moon passes directly between the Earth and the Sun, completely blocking the Sun for a few minutes.


During totality, the sky becomes much darker, bright stars and planets may appear, temperatures can drop, and the Sun's beautiful outer atmosphere, known as the corona, becomes visible.



Northern Spain is one of the best places in Europe to witness this incredible event.


Even if you are outside the path of totality, much of Spain will still enjoy a spectacular partial eclipse.



How to Watch Safely


Never look directly at the Sun without certified eclipse glasses. Ordinary sunglasses are not safe.


If you are inside the path of totality, you may remove your eclipse glasses only while the Sun is completely covered.


As soon as even a small part of the Sun reappears, put your eclipse glasses back on.
