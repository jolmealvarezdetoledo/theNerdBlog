The 2026 Total Solar Eclipse in Spain

Published: August 2026

On August 12, 2026, Spain will experience one of the most spectacular astronomical events in decades: a total solar eclipse.

During a total solar eclipse, the Moon passes directly between the Earth and the Sun, completely blocking the Sun for a short period of time. In the path of totality, daylight suddenly becomes twilight, bright stars may appear, temperatures can drop, and the Sun's outer atmosphere—the corona—becomes visible.

Northern Spain is one of the best places in Europe to witness this event. Even if you are outside the path of totality, much of the country will still enjoy a spectacular partial eclipse.

Viewing the Eclipse Safely

Never look directly at the Sun without certified eclipse glasses (ISO 12312-2). Regular sunglasses do not protect your eyes.

If you are lucky enough to stand inside the path of totality, you may remove your eclipse glasses only during the brief period when the Sun is completely covered. As soon as even a tiny part of the Sun reappears, put your glasses back on.

Why I'm Looking Forward to It

As someone who loves astronomy, this is an event I've been waiting for. I'm hoping for clear skies and I would love to observe the eclipse through my telescope and maybe even take a few photos.

Events like this remind us how amazing our Solar System is. If you have the chance to see the eclipse, don't miss it—it may be a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
